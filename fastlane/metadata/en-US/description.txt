Tartanism is a free, on-device generative tartan studio. Choose patterns you love, breed a new collection, and export your favorites as shareable cards or seamless looping videos.

MAKE A SETT
Generate a wild litter of nine unique plaid patterns. Select the designs you like, tune wildness, and breed a new generation. Keep favorites in your local collection and name them.

SAVE AND SHARE
Export a tartan card as a PNG or a looping 9:16 MP4. On iPhone, save to Photos and open the share sheet to send the actual file. Loops are designed for creator backgrounds and social posts.

COME BACK TOMORROW
A date-seeded daily prompt gives you a fresh starting point. Your collection and progress stay on your device.

Tartanism needs no account, contains no ads or in-app purchases, and does not collect app data. Made by Edgeless Lab.
